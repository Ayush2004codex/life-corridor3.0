Article 06

🚦 Traffic Signal Override Protocol

The Green Corridor Signal Override system operates under strict governance:

  • Authorization: Signal overrides are only initiated upon verified emergency dispatch from an authenticated driver and acknowledged by an authenticated admin.
  • Scope: Overrides affect only signals along the calculated OSRM route between the pickup location and destination hospital.
  • Duration: Overrides are temporary and automatically expire once the ambulance passes the signal zone (800m radius) or the corridor is deactivated.
  • Accountability: Every signal override event is logged with timestamps, coordinates, driver ID, and admin acknowledgment for regulatory audit.
  • Public Safety: The system is designed to minimize disruption to regular traffic flow while maximizing ambulance throughput.
🔵 Life Corridor's signal override system is designed to integrate with existing municipal traffic management infrastructure via standardized APIs. Deployment requires formal agreement with local traffic authorities.
