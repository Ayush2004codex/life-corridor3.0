⚙️ Process

How It Works

Four intelligent steps that save lives — every single time across Kolkata.

1
📞
Emergency Request
Ambulance driver or dispatch triggers an emergency request via the app or direct system call. GPS location is captured instantly anywhere in Kolkata.
2
🤖
AI Route Optimization
Our ML engine analyzes 47 real-time parameters — traffic density, road conditions, signal timings — to compute the fastest route through Kolkata.
3
🚦
Signal Control
Edge controllers override normal signal cycles along the route, creating a continuous green corridor up to 2km ahead of the ambulance.
4
🏥
Hospital Ready
SSKM, RG Kar, NRS receives patient condition, ETA, and required resources 8 minutes before arrival — staff and equipment ready on arrival.
