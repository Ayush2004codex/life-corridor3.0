Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 17, 2026  |  Effective: May 17, 2026

Section 01

🔍 Introduction

Life Corridor ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Life Corridor Smart Green Corridor System — an AI-powered emergency response platform designed to save lives by optimizing ambulance routing and traffic signal management across Kolkata and beyond.


This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our platform, including the Driver Dashboard, Admin Portal, Civilian Tracker, and all associated services. By accessing or using Life Corridor, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

Section 02

📋 Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, improve, and personalize our emergency response services:

  • Personal Identification Data: Name, email address, phone number, role (Driver/Admin), and credentials provided during registration.
  • Location Data: Real-time GPS coordinates of ambulances, pickup locations, and destination hospitals to enable route optimization and green corridor activation.
  • Medical Telemetry: Patient vitals (heart rate, SpO2, blood pressure, respiratory rate, temperature) transmitted from ambulance-mounted IoT sensors for pre-hospital triage.
  • Usage Analytics: Page views, feature interactions, session duration, and device information for platform improvement.
  • Communication Data: Messages exchanged through the AI Chat Assistant within the platform.
⚠️ Critical Note: Medical telemetry data is collected solely for the purpose of emergency medical response and hospital preparation. It is never used for marketing, profiling, or non-medical purposes.
Section 03

⚙️ How We Use Your Information

  • Emergency Dispatch: Routing ambulances to the nearest appropriate hospital and activating green corridor signal overrides.
  • Hospital Preparation: Transmitting patient vitals to destination hospitals so they can prepare ICU beds, surgical teams, and required medical supplies before arrival.
  • Traffic Signal Management: Overriding traffic signals along the ambulance's route to create a safe, unobstructed green corridor.
  • AI-Powered Suggestions: Generating real-time medical preparation recommendations for hospital administrators based on incoming patient data.
  • Platform Security: Role-based access control (RBAC) ensuring only authorized administrators and verified drivers access sensitive dashboards.
  • Analytics & Improvement: Anonymized usage data helps us improve response times and system reliability.
Section 04

🛡️ Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Sensitive data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.
  • Access Control: Strict RBAC ensures drivers cannot access admin dashboards and vice versa. Session tokens are validated on every request.
  • Data Minimization: We collect only the data strictly necessary for emergency response operations.
  • Automatic Purging: Patient medical telemetry data is automatically purged from temporary storage within 24 hours of case closure.
  • Audit Logging: All access to sensitive patient data is logged for compliance and accountability.
🔒 Life Corridor is designed with Privacy by Design principles, ensuring data protection is embedded into every layer of our architecture — not added as an afterthought.
Section 05

🤝 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Data may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • Emergency Services: Patient data is shared with the destination hospital's emergency department for the sole purpose of medical preparation.
  • Routing Services: Anonymous route coordinates are sent to OSRM (Open Source Routing Machine) for route calculation. No personal or medical data is included.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or government regulation.
  • Map Providers: We use CartoDB/OpenStreetMap tile services for map rendering. Only map tile requests (coordinates) are sent — no user data.
Section 06

👤 Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time (may affect service availability).
  • Grievance Redressal: File a complaint with our Data Protection Officer or the relevant supervisory authority.
Section 07

🍪 Cookies & Local Storage

Life Corridor uses localStorage (browser-based storage) for:

  • Session Management: Maintaining your login state and role-based access.
  • Real-Time Sync: Coordinating emergency dispatch data between the Driver and Admin dashboards in real-time.
  • Vital Signs Buffer: Temporarily buffering medical telemetry for display on the hospital preparation dashboard.

We do not use third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies.

Section 08

👶 Children's Privacy

Life Corridor is intended for use by authorized medical professionals, hospital administrators, and certified ambulance drivers. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware that data has been collected from a minor, we will take immediate steps to delete such information.
